ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A process, made visible.

The project illustrates how fluid transport, heating, mixing, and electrostatic separation work together. It also provides context for the evaluation of alternative demulsifiers and flocculants.

Project Objectives

    Engineering Principles

    Transport and conditioning
    Pumps supply hydraulic energy for fluid transport. Preheating reduces crude viscosity, while upstream mixing disperses wash water into the crude.

    Coalescence and separation
    Demulsifier helps weaken the films that stabilize water droplets. The electric field encourages coalescence, and larger droplets settle under gravity while crude is collected above.

    Interface control
    Interface measurement provides feedback for regulating water withdrawal and maintaining the boundary between the lower water phase and the crude above it.

    Process Connections 01

    Crude flows through two successive desalting stages. Water separated in stage 2 is reused upstream of stage 1, creating countercurrent water use between the stages.

    Crude flow

    Raw crude → Feed pump → Preheating → Demulsifier addition → Stage 1 water injection and mixing → Desalter 1 → Stage 2 water injection and mixing → Desalter 2 → Downstream process.

    Water circulation

      Chemical addition

      Demulsifier is introduced into the crude feed before stage 1. Flocculant addition is shown at the oily water tank, before transfer to the Separators Department for further treatment.
