Move & mix

Pump pressure drives flow. Mixing valves disperse wash water into crude before each stage.

02

Coalesce & settle

The electric field helps droplets merge. Larger water drops settle under gravity; oil collects above.

03

Recover & treat

Stage 2 water feeds stage 1. Oily water goes to the Separators Department; treated water returns.
